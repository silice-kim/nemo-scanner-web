Keep and search your barcode scan history.

A scan result can be useful long after the first scan. Nemo keeps previous barcode and QR results available so you can review and search them again later.

Keep Your Scan History with Nemo

The problem

Scanning again is not always the best way to find the same result

If you scan a product code, QR link or asset label today, you may need that result again later. Without history, the only option may be finding and scanning the original code again.

The solution

Save the result once and find it again later

Nemo keeps previous scan results in history. This makes it easier to review earlier scans, search for a saved value and continue working without repeating the original scan.
