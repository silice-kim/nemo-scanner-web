Continuous Barcode Scanner

Scan multiple barcodes without stopping.

If you need to process many barcodes in a row, reopening the scanner after every result slows the workflow. Continuous Scan keeps Nemo ready for the next code.

The problem

Repetitive scanning should not mean repetitive tapping

A normal scan flow often ends after every result. When the job involves dozens of product labels, asset tags or QR codes, repeating the same navigation can interrupt the work.

The solution

Nemo's Continuous Scan mode is designed for repeated scanning. After a result is captured, you can move directly to the next code instead of restarting the scanning process.
